# Refund policy

Effective date: 12.11.2026

*Translation. If the texts differ, the Uzbek text prevails.*

This policy is an integral part of the [Public offer](./public-offer.md) and sets out how funds on a Verificahub Balance are refunded.

## 1. What is refunded

1.1. Unspent Balance is refunded.

1.2. Amounts charged for SMS sent are not refunded, whatever the delivery status: these services are deemed provided.

1.3. Bonus and promo-code credits (if any) are not refunded.

## 2. Time limit

2.1. A refund request must be submitted within 30 (thirty) calendar days of the top-up. Each top-up is counted separately.

2.2. Funds are spent in the order they were added: the earliest top-up is spent first. The refundable amount is the unspent part of top-ups made in the last 30 days.

2.3. Requests made after 30 days are considered where the law requires, including under the consumer rights of individuals.

## 3. How to request a refund

3.1. Email support@verificahub.uz from your Account email address and include:

- your Account email address;
- the payment date and amount, or the transaction ID;
- the amount to refund (optional) and the reason (optional).

3.2. Do not send your card number, expiry date, CVV/CVC, PIN or codes received by SMS. Verificahub will never ask for them.

## 4. Review and refund

4.1. Requests are reviewed within 10 (ten) business days. The approved amount is deducted from the Balance and held during processing.

4.2. Funds are returned to the card used for payment through the payment provider, by cancelling or refunding the transaction. If payment was made by bank transfer, funds are returned to the payer's account.

4.3. The time for funds to reach the card depends on the payment provider and the card issuer.

4.4. Verificahub charges no additional fee for refunds.

## 5. Refusal or limitation

A refund may be refused or limited if:

- the payment is under review for fraud or an unauthorized card transaction;
- a chargeback has been opened for the payment through the bank;
- a breach of section 6 of the Offer caused Verificahub a loss — up to the amount of the loss.

If a chargeback is opened for a payment through the bank, the Account may be suspended until the dispute is resolved ([Public offer](./public-offer.md), clause 9.3). To get a refund, contact support@verificahub.uz first.

## 6. Incorrect charges

Amounts charged incorrectly because of a Platform error are returned to the Balance after review. Report such cases to support@verificahub.uz.

## 7. Account deletion

The remaining Balance is not refunded automatically when an Account is deleted. Submit a refund request before deleting the Account.
