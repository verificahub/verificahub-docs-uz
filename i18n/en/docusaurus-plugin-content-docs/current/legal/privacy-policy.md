# Privacy policy

Effective date: 12.11.2026

*Translation. If the texts differ, the Uzbek text prevails.*

## 1. General

1.1. This Policy sets out how personal data obtained through the Verificahub platform — the verificahub.uz website, the app.verificahub.uz web application, the API, the docs.verificahub.uz documentation and support channels — is processed and protected.

1.2. The Policy follows the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" (ZRU-547) and other legislation.

1.3. **Owner and operator of personal data:** Sole Entrepreneur (YATT) ISKUJIN AYGIZ IRIKOVICH (state registration certificate No. 8125433), Tashkent city, Shaykhontokhur district, Zulfiyaxonim street, 27, 29, 29 AUY ("Verificahub"). Personal data requests: support@verificahub.uz.

1.4. Integrators send their End users' phone numbers for verification. For that data the Integrator is the owner and Verificahub is an operator acting on its instructions. It is processed under the [End-user data processing terms](./data-processing.md).

1.5. Terms have the meaning given in the [Public offer](./public-offer.md).

## 2. Whose data we process

- visitors to verificahub.uz and docs.verificahub.uz;
- people registered on the Platform — Integrators and their representatives;
- people who contact Verificahub.

## 3. What data we process

| Category | Data |
|---|---|
| Registration | first name, last name, email address, password (stored only as a hash) |
| Account verification | +998 phone number; records of accepting the Offer and giving consent (date, time, IP address, document version). If OneID identification is introduced — data provided by OneID that is needed for identification |
| Organization details | name, taxpayer number (STIR), address, representative details — if provided for closing documents |

Verificahub does not request or process special categories of data or biometric data.

## 5. Disclosure to third parties

5.1. **Service providers.** Verificahub may transfer (or otherwise make available) personal data to third parties that help provide the services or provide services on its behalf, for example: accepting and processing payments (banks and payment institutions), sending SMS (SMS gateways and mobile operators), servers and data centers, email delivery and identification services. Personal data may be maintained and processed by these service providers. They receive only the information they need to perform their functions. Verificahub does not authorize them to use or disclose it for their own marketing or other purposes, and requires them to keep it confidential and secure.

5.2. **Government bodies.** Where required by law, data is provided on a lawful request from competent authorities.

5.3. **Business transfers.** If Verificahub's business is reorganized or transferred, data may pass to the successor subject to this Policy.

5.4. **Online advertising.** Verificahub does not share personally identifiable information about individual customers with advertisers. Verificahub may display online advertisements and may share aggregated, non-identifying information about customers, collected through registration, surveys or promotions, with certain advertisers.

5.5. Verificahub does not sell personal data. Disclosure to perform the contract is not dissemination of data.

## 6. Storage location and cross-border transfer

6.1. Platform data, including personal data of citizens of Uzbekistan, is collected, organized and stored on servers in Uzbekistan (Tashkent), as required by ZRU-547.

6.2. Cross-border transfer takes place only on a lawful basis and in compliance with the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage

7.1. verificahub.uz stores your language choice in browser local storage (localStorage).

7.2. The app.verificahub.uz web application uses cookies needed for the sign-in session and security.

## 8. Retention

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account data | while the Account exists; deleted or anonymized within 15 (fifteen) days after the Account is deleted |
| Accounts with an unconfirmed email | 30 (thirty) days after registration |
| Payments, billing, Offer acceptance and consent records | the period required by accounting and tax law |
| Server and API logs (phone numbers masked) | 90 (ninety) days; longer while a security incident is investigated |
| Requests | 1 (one) year after the request is closed |

Data related to an ongoing dispute or investigation is kept until it ends.

## 9. Deleting the Account and withdrawing consent

9.1. You can delete the Account in the web application settings or by writing to support@verificahub.uz.

9.2. To withdraw consent, write to support@verificahub.uz from your Account email address. Processing stops within 15 (fifteen) days, except for data that must be kept by law.

9.3. Withdrawing consent or deleting the Account ends access to the services. Request a refund of the remaining Balance first ([Refund policy](./refund-policy.md)).

## 10. Security

Verificahub applies legal, organizational and technical measures, including:

- TLS encryption for all connections;
- storing passwords only as hashes;
- masking phone numbers in logs and storing them in the database as hashes;
- least-privilege access, key-based authentication, a firewall and monitoring of sign-in attempts;
- backups and an incident response procedure.

No system can guarantee absolute protection, but Verificahub takes reasonable measures appropriate to the risks.

## 12. Your rights

A data subject has the right to:

- know whether their data is processed and receive information about it (purposes, bases, data, periods, recipients);
- request correction, change, completion, blocking or destruction of their data;
- withdraw consent;
- apply to the competent government body or a court.

## 13. How to make a request

13.1. Send your request to support@verificahub.uz. Verificahub may ask for additional information to identify you.

13.2. Requests are handled within the time limits set by law. If a request is refused, the reason is given.

13.3. End users should first contact the relevant Integrator about their data.
