# Consent to personal data processing

Effective date: 12.11.2026

*Translation. If the texts differ, the Uzbek text prevails.*

I, a person registered on the Verificahub platform, by ticking "I consent to the processing of my personal data", in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data", freely, of my own will and in my own interest, give Sole Entrepreneur (YATT) ISKUJIN AYGIZ IRIKOVICH (state registration certificate No. 8125433, Tashkent city, Shaykhontokhur district, Zulfiyaxonim street, 27, 29, 29 AUY; "Verificahub") my consent to process my personal data on the following terms.

## 1. Data

- first name, last name, email address;
- phone number;
- if OneID identification is introduced — data provided by OneID that is needed for identification;
- details of the organization I represent and of my authority (if I provide them);
- payment data (amount, date, transaction ID);
- IP address, browser and device data, logs of my actions on the Platform;
- the content of my requests and correspondence.

## 2. Purposes

- registration, identification and Account verification;
- entering into and performing the [Public offer](./public-offer.md), billing;
- sending service messages, including from no-reply@verificahub.uz;
- security and prevention of fraud and abuse;
- handling requests;
- complying with the law.

## 3. Actions

Collection, recording, organization, storage, clarification, modification, completion, use, transfer, anonymization, blocking, deletion and destruction — with or without automated means.

## 4. Disclosure

Under section 5 of the [Privacy policy](./privacy-policy.md), my data may be disclosed to parties that help Verificahub provide the services (payments, SMS delivery, servers, email and identification services), only as needed for these purposes, and to competent government bodies where required by law.

My Account data is stored on servers in Uzbekistan.

## 5. Duration and withdrawal

5.1. This consent is valid while my Account exists and for the retention periods required by law.

5.2. I may withdraw consent by writing to support@verificahub.uz or by deleting my Account. Processing stops within 15 (fifteen) days, except for data that must be kept by law.

5.3. I understand that withdrawing consent ends my access to the services.

## 6. I confirm that

- I have read the [Privacy policy](./privacy-policy.md);
- the data I provide is accurate;
- if I provide other people's data (for example, my organization's employees), I have their consent.

Consent is given electronically by ticking the box. Verificahub keeps the date, time, IP address and the version of the document consented to.

This consent does not include consent to receive advertising messages.
